## 🌟 Introduction
Welcome to **Swastik**! This platform was built specifically to bridge the healthcare gap in remote and underserved areas, focusing on joint and bone health—specifically **Osteoarthritis** and **Osteoporosis**. 

Unlike traditional medical software, this platform is not designed for specialist doctors. Instead, it is tailored for two specific audiences: **Remote Dispensary Technicians** and **Patients** themselves.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. 🎓 Remote Technician Education & Certification
In many rural areas, fully qualified specialists are unavailable, and initial care falls to local dispensary technicians. Swastik serves as an evidence-based medical education hub for these technicians.

* **Targeted Curriculum:** Comprehensive, easy-to-understand modules covering the symptoms, progression, and basic care strategies for Osteoarthritis and Osteoporosis.
* **Proctored Mastery Exam:** Technicians undergo a strict, anti-cheat assessment to test their clinical understanding of the material.
* **Cryptographic Certification:** Upon passing the exam, technicians are awarded an official **Certificate of Clinical Mastery**. 
  * *The certificate is generated with premium national-crest aesthetics and features an interactive **Blockchain Proof** drawer that verifies the technician's active study time and exam integrity (e.g., tracking tab switches).*

### 2. 🏥 AI-Powered Patient Dashboard
Once a patient is onboarded, they gain access to a highly accessible, personalized health dashboard designed for the elderly and those with limited mobility.

* **Tri-Layer Voice Assistant Form:** Recognizing that elderly patients may struggle with typing, the dashboard features a voice-first pain journal. Patients can simply speak naturally in their native language (Hindi, English, Bengali, etc.), and the AI automatically extracts their pain level, symptoms, and updates their journal.
* **Longitudinal Pain History:** A visual calendar and graphed history of the patient's pain trends, making it incredibly easy to present their progress to a doctor during their next visit.
* **AI Health Insights:** The dashboard leverages Google Gemini to continuously analyze the patient's historical pain entries, providing encouraging, personalized insights and identifying symptom triggers.
* **Media Uploads:** Patients can attach audio notes or pictures of localized swelling directly to their pain logs.
* **Multi-Lingual RAG Chatbot:** A personalized AI assistant that is aware of the patient's medical journal history. It can answer general questions about joint health while referencing the patient's specific past symptoms with empathy.
